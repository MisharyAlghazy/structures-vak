This report presents the answers to the MT44030 \emph{Advanced Mechanics of Maritime Structures} assignment (2026 edition). The chapters of this report follow the structure of the assignment brief directly: \textbf{Part~1} (Chapters~\ref{ch:assignment1}--\ref{ch:assignment3}) contains Assignments~1--3, and \textbf{Part~2} (Chapters~\ref{ch:assignment4}--\ref{ch:assignment7}) contains Assignments~4--7. \section{Course objectives}

\begin{itemize}
  \item To model free warping torsion of beam-like structural members mathematically, and determine the stiffness, strength characteristics, and displacements for solid and hollow cross-sections.
  \item To model constrained warping torsion of beam-like structural members mathematically, and calculate the induced shear and normal stresses for hollow cross-sections.
  \item To establish the generalized beam torsion equation for hollow cross-sections.
  \item To identify the torsion load-carrying members and their efficiency in marine structures at both local and global levels.
  \item To calculate the torsion loading-induced response of marine structures for analysis and design purposes.
  \item To familiarize with measurement and monitoring of the torsion loading-induced response of marine structures.
\end{itemize}

\section{Vessel and idealised geometry}

To approximate the structural layout of a shortsea bulk carrier, the total layout is divided into five blocks of three cross-section types, as shown schematically in the assignment brief (Figures~1--2): section~A (aft, engine room, closed section), section~B (parallel midship, appearing three times, holds with open or closed hatch covers), and section~C (bow, closed section, similar shape to~A but with a reduced equivalent width to account for the tapered bow).

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Section A} contains the engine room of the vessel and is a closed section, approximated by a hollow triangle (Assignment~1).
  \item \textbf{Section B} occurs three times in the vessel and comprises the holds. The holds are usually open but can be closed using hatch covers (Assignments~2--5).
  \item \textbf{Section C} is the bow of the vessel; for this course it is assumed to be a closed section of similar shape to Section~A, with a smaller equivalent width (Assignment~5).
\end{itemize}
